How your cue sheet data stays private

Your rehearsal plan stays in your browser. We do not receive its title, section labels, players, risks, measures, or tempo targets.

Data on your device

The app stores your current cue sheet in IndexedDB so it survives refreshes and works offline. If you restore a paid license, the license token and a dated verification result are stored in localStorage. You can remove this data through your browser’s site-data controls. JSON and CSV exports are files you control.

Checkout and license verification

If you choose Conductor unlock, checkout is hosted by the Sociobot billing service and Dodo acts as merchant of record. Their checkout processes the contact and payment information needed for the transaction. This app sends only the license token to Sociobot when checking whether an unlock is active. It does not send your cue-sheet content.

Network requests

The installed app may request its own files to load or update. It may contact api.sociobot.in for checkout or license verification. There are no advertising trackers, third-party fonts, analytics scripts, cloud score storage, or user accounts in v1. Standard hosting logs may temporarily contain IP address, request path, and browser information for security and operations.

Your choices

You can use every planning, accessibility, offline, import, and export feature without buying or creating an account. You can export a backup at any time and delete local data through browser settings.

Questions

For privacy or data requests, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
